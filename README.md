Calls & Puts: It displays both Call options (bets that the stock will go up) on the left, and Put options (bets that the stock will go down) on the right.

Columns: For both Calls and Puts, it shows the Bid price, Ask price, Last traded price, Volume (how many traded today), IV (Implied Volatility), and Open Interest (how many contracts are currently open).

2. Strategy Panel (Top Right)

The Legs:

Sell: A $260 Call expiring on 2026-01-16 (The yellow highlighted row in the table).

Buy: A $265 Call expiring on 2026-01-16 (The orange highlighted row in the table).

Trade Metrics:

Net Credit: $1.85. This means you receive $185 upfront per contract for placing this trade.

Max Profit: $185.00 (You keep the credit if the stock stays below $260).

Max Loss: -$315.00 (If the stock goes above $265, you lose the difference between the strikes minus the credit).

3. Analysis Charts (Bottom)

Profit / Loss (P&L) Chart: This shows your profit or loss based on the price of AAPL at expiration. The blue line shows that if AAPL is below $260, you make a small profit ($185). If it goes above $265, you hit your maximum loss (-$315). The red shaded area represents the "loss zone."
